# Privacy Policy — agol2drive

agol2drive is an internal automation tool for AdHoc Geo Marketing Research.
It copies photo attachments from the operator's ArcGIS Online feature layer
into the operator's own Google Drive and OneDrive, then writes the folder
links back to the layer.

- Google scope used: drive.file (access limited to files the app creates).
- Data is stored only in the operator's own Drive/OneDrive. Nothing is shared
  with third parties and nothing is sold.
- Contact: adhocfieldphoto@gmail.com
